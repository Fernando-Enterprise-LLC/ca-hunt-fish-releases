CA Hunt & Fish Guide — Support

Published by Antonio Esquivel, an individual developer.

Found wrong or outdated regulation data?

That is the most important thing you can report. Please email svpguru@gmail.com or open an issue with the water or zone name and what you saw — every value in the app carries a "verified as of" date and a link to its official source, which is what makes a report actionable. Reports are read. In the meantime, rely on the official source linked from that screen.

App problems, questions, feature requests

GitHub issues is the fastest channel for those too, and email works for anything you would rather not post in public.

Subscriptions and billing

The app is free. CA Hunt & Fish Pro is an optional subscription, sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account, at $34.99 per year or $5.99 per month. It renews automatically for another term of the same length until you cancel, and there is no minimum term and no cancellation fee.

To cancel, either route works and neither has any step beyond these:

  • In the app, open Settings and tap "Manage or cancel subscription".
  • On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose CA Hunt & Fish Pro, and cancel there.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the term you have already paid for, and you keep Pro until then. If you are in a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Questions about a subscription may be sent to svpguru@gmail.com, or by mail to Antonio Esquivel, 7410 Alpine Boulevard, Alpine, CA 91901-2276.

The full subscription terms are in section 14 of the Terms of Use, and what the app stores is described in the Privacy Policy.
